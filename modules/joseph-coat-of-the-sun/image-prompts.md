# Image prompts: Joseph – The Coat of the Sun storybook scenes

Paste each prompt into an image generator. Each is self-contained (style line included). Target: 16:10 landscape, ideally 320x192 native pixel grid, then upscale with nearest-neighbour. Cover: 1200x630.

**Shared style (inside every prompt):** detailed 16-bit pixel art, dithered gradients, limited palette, 3-4 tone shading per object, no outlines, no anti-aliasing, no text, warm reverent children's storybook mood.

**Character sheet (keep consistent across all images):**
- **Joseph:** young boy (≈ 12–17 yrs, adult-looking only from Stage 9), short dark-brown hair, olive skin, rosy cheeks. Costume by phase: *Sun Coat* (long-sleeved golden-orange tunic with 12 small sunray stripes, Stages 1–4) → *plain grey-brown slave tunic* (Stages 5–7) → *fine white linen robe, gold collar, signet ring* (Stages 8–12).
- **Jacob:** old, long white beard, brown-and-cream robe, walking staff. **Brothers:** dark-haired, wool tunics of earth tones. **Benjamin:** small child (hence the youngest). **Pharaoh:** striped blue-and-gold headcloth, gentle dignified face. Egyptian decor is plain and decorative only (lotus pillars, papyrus, geometric patterns); no worship scenes.
- Never depict violence, blood, or scary faces; the "blood" on the coat is only a deep-red stain.

**Negative prompt (if supported):** smooth gradients, blur, vector, 3D render, photo, text, watermark, outlines, realistic faces, scary, gore, blood, nudity, modern clothing, pagan ritual.

## 0. Prologue (`intro-start.png`)

Detailed 16-bit pixel art illustration, 16:10 landscape, dithered gradients, limited palette, 3-4 tone shading per object, no outlines, no anti-aliasing, no text, warm reverent children's storybook mood. A cozy evening in a child's room: a honey-brown wooden desk with a glowing brass oil lamp, an open Bible, a torn parchment map in three pieces (hills of Hebron, the Nile, a green land of Goshen), and a long-sleeved golden-orange coat with twelve sunray stripes folded over the chair. Rising gently from the Bible's pages is a small golden vision of a wheat sheaf, the sun, the moon and eleven stars, a granary and a white dove. Warm, peaceful, adventurous.

## 1. The Dream of the Sheaves (`intro-1.png`)

Same style as above. Genesis 37:1-11: golden wheat fields of Hebron at sunrise. In the centre young Joseph in a golden-orange long-sleeved coat stands beside one tall sheaf, while eleven sheaves around it lean and bow. In the upper sky sit a bright sun, a pale moon and eleven stars in a gentle arc. On the left, his ten older brothers in earth-tone tunics look at him with arms crossed and uneasy faces; on the right old Jacob with a white beard leans on his staff, thoughtful and tender. Dreamlike violet edge to the sky, hopeful and innocent.

## 2. The Road to Shechem and Dothan (`intro-2.png`)

Same style. Genesis 37:12-17: a winding dusty hill road in the Judean hills at midday with a fork of three paths and cairns of stacked stones. Young Joseph in the golden-orange coat carries a bread bag and a water jug, looking at a kindly stranger in a cream cloak who points toward a distant valley with Dothan's tents and sheep as tiny dots. Wildflowers, olive trees, warm hopeful light, a long road ahead.

## 3. The Dry Well at Dothan (`intro-3.png`)

Same style. Genesis 37:18-28, gentle, no violence: a dry stone well on a pasture hill, seen from above. At the bottom of the well sits young Joseph looking up toward a round patch of blue sky, praying calmly, the golden-orange coat gone (only a small glow around him). Above on the rim: Reuben leans over with a worried face and a hidden rope; Judah points toward a faraway dust cloud where an Ishmaelite caravan of camels approaches; other brothers stand uneasily holding the coat, which has a dark-red stain. Dusky, tense yet tender light.

## 4. The Caravan in the Desert (`intro-4.png`)

Same style. Genesis 37:25-36: a long desert caravan of camels with spice bags crossing orange dunes toward the setting sun. Joseph in a plain brown tunic sits behind a camel looking back at a tiny faraway hill with a small figure of old Jacob holding the golden-orange coat in his arms (a small separate vignette in the upper left, framed by a cloud). Night is coming; first stars appear. Sorrowful yet hopeful; a tiny golden light glows over Joseph's heart.

## 5. Potiphar's House (`intro-5.png`)

Same style. Genesis 39:1-6: a sunny Egyptian courtyard with a lotus pond, papyrus plants, painted geometric columns. Joseph in a plain grey-brown tunic carries a jar and a basket, a thin golden glow around his shoulders; Potiphar, a dignified Egyptian officer in white linen, smiles with approval and hands him a ring of keys; servants in the background. Orderly, warm, trustworthy.

## 6. The Corridor of the Open Door (`intro-6.png`)

Same style. Genesis 39:7-20, child-appropriate (no romance): a long stone corridor with columns, one door on the left glowing red, a great open door at the end flooded with white light. Joseph runs toward the light with a determined face, leaving his outer coat in a shadowy hand; behind him a dark, tall shadow-silhouette (representing the temptation to do wrong) fades away. Dramatic yet gentle light-vs-shadow, strong pixel contrast.

## 7. The Prison Window (`intro-7.png`)

Same style. Genesis 40: a stone prison cell with a narrow high window whose beam of sunlight falls like stairs. Joseph in grey-brown tunic sits calmly between two Egyptian officials (a chief cupbearer and a chief baker). Floating in two thought-bubble-style dream clouds: left, three vines of grapes squeezed into a cup; right, three baskets of bread with birds. Quiet, patient, compassionate.

## 8. Pharaoh's Dream of Seven Cows (`intro-8.png`)

Same style. Genesis 41:1-36: a majestic Egyptian palace hall with lotus columns. On the throne sits Pharaoh in blue-and-gold striped headcloth, listening with surprise. Behind the throne a dream vision: seven fat cows rising from the Nile eaten by seven thin cows, and seven full ears of grain beside seven thin ears. In front, young Joseph in a plain white robe (just freed) bows humbly and points upward to heaven. Golden light, wonder and humility.

## 9. The Governor and the Granaries (`intro-9.png`)

Same style. Genesis 41:37-49: sunlit banks of the Nile. Pharaoh gently places a signet ring on Joseph, who now wears a fine white linen robe with a gold collar. In the background on rolling green farmland rows of mud-brick granaries rise, with people carrying sacks of grain, a sailboat on the Nile. A white dove above. Triumphant yet humble, golden warm palette.

## 10. The Famine and the Brothers (`intro-10.png`)

Same style. Genesis 41:53 – 42:38: a dusty plaza before large granaries during famine; a long line of poor people and children receiving grain from workers. In the foreground ten brothers in travel-worn tunics kneel with bowed heads before the governor Joseph, who sits dignified on a raised seat, hiding his emotion with a hand on his heart; an Egyptian interpreter stands beside him. Subtle tears glimmer in Joseph's eyes. Solemn, tender.

## 11. The Silver Cup and Judah's Plea (`intro-11.png`)

Same style. Genesis 43–44: a banquet hall with a long table, torches and lotus pillars. In the foreground an open sack of grain with a small shining silver cup; young Benjamin looks frightened. Judah kneels before Joseph with hands joined, offering himself in the child's place; the other brothers behind watch in sorrow. Joseph's face softens, a single tear glows. Intense, moving.

## 12. Reunion in Goshen (`intro-12.png`)

Same style. Genesis 45–46 and 50: green pastures of Goshen at golden sunrise with Egyptian wagons and tents. Joseph in white linen runs to embrace old Jacob; brothers embrace one another with tears and smiles; Benjamin is lifted by Reuben. In the centre sky the golden-orange sun-coat (now mended with golden thread) floats like a radiant sun over all of them; a dove above. Joyful, forgiving, family-warm.

## 13. Epilogue: Joseph and Jesus (`intro-end.png`)

Same style. A cozy evening in a child's room: desk, lamp, open Bible, the mended golden-orange coat spread like a sun on the desk. Rising above the Bible, a small gentle vision of Joseph in a white robe on the left and Jesus the Good Shepherd on the right holding a lamb; between them a small pouch of silver coins and a loaf of bread glowing softly, and a white dove. Peaceful, reverent, forgiving.
